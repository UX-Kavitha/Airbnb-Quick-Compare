A Product Design Case Study

Airbnb Quick Compare

Accelerating group travel decisions to improve booking conversion

Kavitha Sankaran · Product Designer Duration: 8 weeks (Feb 2026)

How Three Friends Book a Stay in 60 Seconds - Watch Video

Planning a trip with friends sounds exciting until it comes to choosing where to stay.

While Airbnb allows users to save homes, it lacks a structured way to compare shortlisted options. This leads to cognitive overload, delayed group alignment, and missed booking opportunities.

To solve this, I introduced Quick Compare - a feature that transforms the Wishlist into a decision-making layer, enabling users to view stays side by side, align faster with their group, and book with confidence.

This case study explores an 8-week solo UX concept project, completed as part of the UX Gym Ignite program, where I redesigned the Airbnb Wishlist experience to address this exact problem.

This project reflects a user-centered approach to reducing decision friction and designing for real-world group behavior in travel planning, and was recognized with a Strategic Product Thinking Award and an Overall Best Performer Award.
